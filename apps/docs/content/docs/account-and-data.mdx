---
title: Account and data
description: Export everything, delete a workspace, or delete your account.
---

## Export your data (owners)

**Settings → Data**:

- **Download ZIP** — one spreadsheet (CSV) per kind of record: clients, quotes, invoices and lines, payments, receipts, credit notes, contracts, projects, time, expenses, members and the audit log.
- **Email me a ZIP with PDFs** — the same, plus a PDF of every issued invoice, quote, receipt and contract. It's built in the background and emailed to you as a link that works for 3 days.

## Delete a workspace (owners)

Also in **Settings → Data**. Type the workspace's web address to confirm. Every record and file is deleted for all members; member accounts stay. A paid plan ends and isn't refunded. Download your data first — this can't be undone.

## Delete your account

**Account → Delete account**. You can't delete your account while you're the only owner of a workspace — make someone else an owner, or delete that workspace first.

## Audit log

**Settings → Audit log** lists important actions — who did what and when, including actions taken with API keys.
