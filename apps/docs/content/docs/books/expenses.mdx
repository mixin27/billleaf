## Recording an expense

**Expenses → New expense**:

- **Date, vendor and amount** — the total you paid, tax included.
- **Of which tax** — optional; used for the [tax summary](/docs/books/reports#tax-summary).
- **Category** — ten common ones are ready to use; add or hide your own with **Categories**.
- **Currency, how you paid, reference and notes**.
- **Client and project** — if the expense was for a client.
- **Receipt** — attach a photo (PNG or JPEG) or a PDF, up to 5 MB.

The list can be filtered by category and billing status and shows totals per currency.

On the go, record an expense from the [mobile app](/docs/getting-started/mobile-app#expenses-and-receipts) with a photo of the receipt.

## Billing expenses to a client

Tick **Bill this to the client** on an expense with a client. It then appears under **Expenses to bill** on the client's page. Click **Invoice …** to create a draft invoice with one line per expense, at its full amount — one invoice per currency.

Expenses on an invoice are locked (the amount and client can't change). Deleting the draft or voiding the invoice makes them billable again.
