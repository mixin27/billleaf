**Reports** shows how the business is doing for a period — this year, last year, the last 12 months, this or last month, or your own dates.

## Profit & loss

- **Income** — payments you received, minus refunds you made.
- **Expenses** — what you recorded under [Expenses](/docs/books/expenses).
- **Profit** — the difference, per month and in total.

Each currency is shown separately; nothing is converted. **Export CSV** downloads the monthly figures.

## Expenses by category

Where your money goes, with each category's share.

## Tax summary

- **Charged on invoices** — tax on invoices issued in the period, minus tax given back by credit notes.
- **Paid on expenses** — the tax part of your expenses.
- **Difference** — a guide to what you may owe.

This is a helper for you and your accountant, not a tax return.
