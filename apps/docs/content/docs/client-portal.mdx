Every document you email includes a link to the client portal: a private page where your client sees everything you've sent them, in your branding.

## Signing in

There are no passwords for clients. The link in your email signs them in; later they can ask for a new link with their email address on the portal's sign-in page. Links are personal and expire.

## What clients can do

- **Quotes** — read, download, **accept** (typing their name) or **decline** with a reason.
- **Contracts** — read and **sign** online.
- **Invoices** — see what's owed and [how to pay](/docs/getting-paid/payment-methods), download the PDF, and upload a [payment proof](/docs/getting-paid/payment-proofs).
- **Receipts** — download receipts for their payments.

Clients only ever see their own documents, and only once they've been sent or issued — never drafts.
