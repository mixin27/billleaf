---
title: Credit notes
description: Correct or partly refund an issued invoice without editing it.
---

Use a credit note when an issued invoice needs a correction — a discount agreed afterwards, a cancelled item, or a refund.

<Steps>
<Step>
Open the invoice → **More → Issue credit note**.
</Step>
<Step>
Credit **the whole invoice**, or **part of it** (a description, amount and tax rate), and give a reason. The preview shows how it will apply.
</Step>
<Step>
Issue it. It's numbered (for example `CN-0003`), appears on the invoice, and can be emailed to the client as a PDF.
</Step>
</Steps>

## How the credit applies

A credit first lowers what the client **still owes** on the invoice. If they've already paid, the rest is shown as a **refund due** — refund it through your bank or wallet (or agree to keep it as credit). You can't credit more than the invoice total minus earlier credit notes.

## Undoing

Credit notes can't be edited. **Void** one to undo it; the invoice's balance goes back up. An invoice can't be voided while it has credit notes — void those first. All credit notes are listed under **Invoices → Credit notes**.
