## From draft to issued

<Steps>
<Step>
**Invoices → New invoice** (or convert an accepted quote, or invoice a project's time or a client's expenses). Add lines with quantity, price and tax rate. The issue date defaults to today; the due date comes from your payment terms.
</Step>
<Step>
**Issue** it. Billleaf gives it the next number with no gaps (for example `INV-0042`) and freezes your details and the client's on it.
</Step>
<Step>
**Send** it by email. The client gets the PDF, your payment instructions and a link to view it in the [client portal](/docs/client-portal).
</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout title="Issued invoices can't be edited" type="warn">
That keeps your records trustworthy. To correct an issued invoice, issue a [credit note](/docs/getting-paid/credit-notes), or — if nothing has been paid — **void** it and issue a new one.
</Callout>

## Statuses

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Draft** | Not issued yet; editable, no number. |
| **Open** | Issued, nothing paid yet, not past due. |
| **Partially paid** | Some of it is paid or credited. |
| **Overdue** | Past its due date and not fully settled. |
| **Paid** | Paid (and/or credited) in full. |
| **Void** | Cancelled. Kept for your records; its number isn't reused. |

Amounts are kept per currency. **Home** shows what you're owed, how overdue it is, and payments received this month.
