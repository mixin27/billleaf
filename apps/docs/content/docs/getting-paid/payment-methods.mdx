---
title: Payment methods
description: Tell clients how to pay you — bank accounts, wallets and QR codes.
---

In **Settings → Payment methods**, add the ways clients can pay you:

- **Bank account** — bank name, account name and number.
- **Mobile wallet** — for example KBZPay, WavePay or AYA Pay, with the number and name.
- **Other** — anything else, with your own instructions.

Upload a **QR code** image for a method if you have one, and choose which currencies it's for (leave empty for all). Use **Move up** and **Move down** to set the order.

Active methods that match an invoice's currency appear:

- in a **How to pay** section on the invoice PDF,
- in the invoice email,
- on the invoice in the client portal, where clients can copy numbers and enlarge QR codes.

If none of your active methods take an invoice's currency, for example a USD invoice when your only methods are MMK wallets, the client sees no payment details anywhere. The invoice page warns you (**Your client won't see how to pay this invoice**) on drafts and on issued invoices that are still owed, and so does the send dialog and the invoice screen in the mobile app. Add a method for that currency, or one with no currencies set, and the warning goes away.
