---
title: Payment proofs
description: Clients upload a screenshot of their transfer; you confirm it in one click.
---

When a client pays by bank transfer or wallet, they can tell you straight away:

<Steps>
<Step>
In the client portal, on the invoice, they click **I've paid**, enter the amount, date and transaction ID, and upload a screenshot.
</Step>
<Step>
You get an email, and the proof appears on the invoice and under **Payments to confirm** on the Payments page (and on **Home**).
</Step>
<Step>
Check it against your bank or wallet, then:
- **Confirm** — records the payment (you can adjust the amount or date) and, if you like, emails the client a receipt.
- **Reject** — with a short reason. The client is emailed and can upload a new proof.
</Step>
</Steps>

Screenshots are stored privately and count towards your storage.
