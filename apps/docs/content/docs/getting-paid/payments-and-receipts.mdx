---
title: Payments and receipts
description: Record payments against invoices and send receipts.
---

On an issued invoice, **Record payment**: amount, date, method and reference. A payment can't be more than what's still owed. Each payment gets a numbered **receipt** (for example `RCT-0012`) that you can download or email to the client.

When the full amount is paid (or credited), the invoice becomes **Paid**.

Away from your desk? Record payments from the [mobile app](/docs/getting-started/mobile-app#invoices-and-payments).

## Refunds

Refunding a payment marks it refunded, voids its receipt and puts the amount back on the invoice. Refund the money itself through your bank or wallet.

All payments are listed under **Payments**, where you can filter and search.
