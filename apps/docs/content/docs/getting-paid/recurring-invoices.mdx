---
title: Recurring invoices
description: Bill retainers and subscriptions automatically (Pro and Team).
---

**Invoices → Recurring → New schedule**: pick the client, lines and currency, then how often — every N **weeks**, **months** or **years** — and when to start (and optionally end).

Choose what happens each period:

- **Create a draft** — you review and issue it yourself, or
- **Issue and send** — the invoice is issued and emailed automatically.

Billleaf creates one invoice per period and never doubles up. Monthly schedules starting on the 31st use the last day of shorter months. Pause a schedule any time; its history lists every invoice it created.
