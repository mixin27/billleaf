---
title: Payment reminders
description: Let Billleaf nudge clients about unpaid invoices.
---

In **Settings → General → Payment reminders**, pick when clients are emailed about an unpaid invoice, relative to its due date:

**7, 3 or 1 day before · on the day · 1, 3, 7, 14 or 30 days after**

Each reminder goes out once per invoice, to the person the invoice was last sent to (or the client's email), with a link to view and pay it. Reminders stop when the invoice is paid, credited in full or voided.

To skip reminders for one invoice — for example a client who agreed to pay late — use **Pause reminders** on that invoice. Its page also lists the reminders already sent.
