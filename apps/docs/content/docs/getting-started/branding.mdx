title: Business profile and branding
description: The details and look that appear on your documents.
---

Everything under **Settings → General** is copied onto your documents.

## Business details

- **Name and legal name** — the legal name is used on documents when set.
- **Email, phone, address and tax ID** — shown in the "From" block of quotes, invoices and receipts.
- **Default currency** — used for new clients, quotes and invoices. Each client and document can use its own currency; amounts are never converted.
- **Time zone** — decides what "today" and "overdue" mean for you.
- **Payment terms** — the number of days before an invoice is due. Clients can have their own terms.
- **Default tax rate** — prefilled on new document lines.

## Logo and colours (Pro and Team)

Upload a PNG or JPEG logo (up to 1 MB) and pick a brand colour. Both appear on PDFs, emails and the client portal. On the Free plan documents use a neutral style and show a small "Made with Billleaf" note.

<Callout title="Issued documents keep their details">
When you issue an invoice or send a quote, Billleaf takes a snapshot of your details and the client's. Changing your address later doesn't change documents you've already issued.
</Callout>
