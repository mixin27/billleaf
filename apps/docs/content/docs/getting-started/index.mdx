title: Create your workspace
description: Sign up, create a workspace and find your way around.
---

A **workspace** is your business in Billleaf: its clients, documents, payments and team. You can belong to several workspaces (for example your own business and a client's studio) and switch between them from the menu at the top of the sidebar.

<Steps>
<Step>
### Sign up

Create an account with your email and a password, then confirm your email address from the message we send you.
</Step>
<Step>
### Create a workspace

Give it your business name and a short web address (for example `acme-studio`). The address appears in links, so pick something you're happy for clients to see.
</Step>
<Step>
### Fill in your business profile

Go to **Settings → General** and add your legal name, address, tax ID, default currency, time zone and payment terms. These appear on every quote, invoice and receipt. See [Business profile and branding](/docs/getting-started/branding).
</Step>
<Step>
### Add how clients pay you

In **Settings → Payment methods**, add your bank accounts or mobile wallets (with a QR code if you have one). They're printed on invoices and shown to clients online. See [Payment methods](/docs/getting-paid/payment-methods).
</Step>
<Step>
### Add your first client and quote

Create a client, send them a quote, and you're on your way.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Finding your way around

The sidebar follows the journey:

- **Sell** — Clients, Quotes, Contracts
- **Work** — Projects, Time
- **Get paid** — Invoices, Recurring, Payments
- **Books** — Expenses, Reports

Press <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd> (or <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd>) anywhere to search and jump to any page. **Home** shows what needs your attention: what you're owed, what's overdue, quotes waiting for an answer, payment proofs to review and project budgets.
