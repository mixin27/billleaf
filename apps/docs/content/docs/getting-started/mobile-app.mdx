## Signing in

Sign in with the same email and password you use on the web. Tap the eye at the end of the password to check what you typed. If your account has [two-factor authentication](/docs/getting-started/security#two-factor-authentication), the app asks for the 6-digit code from your authenticator app. No phone to hand? Tap **Use a backup code instead** and enter one of your backup codes.

- **New to Billleaf?** **Create an account** opens the web sign-up. Verify your email and create your workspace there, then sign in in the app.
- **Forgot your password?** **Forgot password?** opens the [web reset page](/docs/getting-started/security#forgot-your-password).

You stay signed in until you sign out. The app keeps your session in your phone's secure storage (the Keychain on iPhone).

## Workspaces

If you belong to one workspace, the app opens it. With several, pick one; the app remembers it next time. Tap the workspace name at the top of the home screen or **More** (or **More → Workspaces**) to switch. To create a workspace, use **Create a workspace on the web**.

## Getting around

The bar at the bottom has **Home**, **Money**, **Time** and **More**, with the round **timer** button in the middle. You see what your [role](/docs/getting-started/team) and plan allow:

- **More** holds everything else: **Invoices**, **Quotes** and **Contracts** under **Sales**, **Clients**, **Projects** and **Timesheet approvals** under **Work**, and your **Account**, **Workspaces** and **Sign out** under **You**.
- Without time tracking, there's no timer button and **Time**'s place goes to **Invoices** (or **Clients**).
- On a tablet the bar becomes a rail down the left side with every section on it, and the app turns with the tablet. Phones stay upright.

## Offline and connection problems

The app keeps what you last loaded on your phone, so it opens straight away and you can still look things up without a connection:

- While you're offline, a small **Offline** notice shows at the top. Screens show what was last loaded and refresh by themselves when you're back online. Pull to refresh does nothing until then.
- Saving (an entry, a payment, starting the timer) needs a connection. Offline, the app says so and nothing is saved; try again once you're back online.
- If something can't load, the screen says why in plain words (offline, Billleaf can't be reached, a problem on Billleaf's side) with **Try again** when that can help.
- If your session ends (you signed out everywhere, or changed your password on the web), the app says so and takes you to sign-in.

Signing out removes the saved data from your phone.

## Home

Home is where you start the day. Pull down to refresh. You only see what your [role](/docs/getting-started/team) and plan allow:

- **Cash on hand**: what's in all your [cashbooks](/docs/books/cashbook), one currency per page (swipe for the next), with this month's cash in and out. Tap it to open **Money**. If you don't use cashbooks but track time, it shows your week instead.
- **Quick actions**: one tap to **Cash in**, **Cash out**, start the **Timer**, **Log time**, add an **Expense**, a **New book**, or open **Invoices**, **Quotes**, **Clients** or **Approvals**. Cash in and Cash out open the entry form on your book, or ask which book when you have several. Tap **Edit** to choose up to eight and put them in order; **Reset** goes back to the defaults. The choice is kept on your phone.
- **Needs attention**: only what's waiting on you, such as overdue invoices, payment proofs to review, timesheets to approve, quotes about to expire, or your week sent back. Tap a row to open it. The card disappears when there's nothing to do.
- **Your time**: what you've tracked and how much is billable, for **This week**, **This month** or **This year** (tap the period to change it), with a bar per day (per month for the year) and your top three projects. It's always your own time, even if you can see everyone's on the web.
- **Owed to you**: what clients owe, per currency, with how late it is (not yet due, 1–90 days, 90+ days). Tap it to open your unpaid invoices.
- **Received**: payments this month and last month, with the last six months.
- **Your books**: your three most recently used books with their balances.

Amounts use your workspace's language and region, like your invoices.

## Account

Tap your initials at the top right of the home screen (or **More → Account**) to open **Account**:

- Your name and email, and the workspace you're in with its plan. **Switch** changes workspace.
- **Appearance**: **System** follows your phone's light or dark mode; **Light** or **Dark** keeps the app that way.
- **Notifications**: turn on [notifications](#notifications) and choose which kinds you get.
- **Timer reminder**: when the [forgotten timer reminder](#forgotten-timer-reminder) fires, or **Off**.
- **Profile and security** opens your account settings on the web (name, password, [two-factor authentication](/docs/getting-started/security#two-factor-authentication) and sessions). Your phone's browser may ask you to sign in first.
- **Sign out**: the app asks first. Signing out removes your session, the remembered workspace and any timer reminder from the phone, and the phone stops getting notifications. Your data stays in Billleaf.

## Notifications

The app can tell you when something happens that you'd otherwise check for:

- **Quote accepted or declined**: a client answered a [quote](/docs/selling/quotes) in the client portal, or a teammate recorded the answer.
- **Contract signed**: a [contract](/docs/selling/contracts) was signed.
- **Payment received or proof of payment sent**: a payment was recorded on an invoice, or a client sent proof of a payment from the portal.
- **Timesheet submitted for approval**: a teammate submitted a [timesheet](/docs/work/timesheet-approvals) you can approve.
- **Your timesheet approved or sent back**: an approver reviewed a week you submitted. Only you are told, with the approver's note if there is one.

Tap a notification to open the quote, contract, invoice or timesheet it's about. One about your own week opens the **Time** tab on that week. If it's from another workspace, the app switches to it first. Anything you have open that it changes refreshes by itself.

To turn notifications on, open **Account** and tap **Turn on notifications**, then allow them when your phone asks. The app doesn't ask when you first open it. If you said no before, **Open Settings** takes you to your phone's settings, where you can allow notifications for Billleaf.

Under **Notifications** on **Account**, switch off any kind you don't want. Your choices are saved on your account, so they apply on all your phones and in every workspace. A kind marked **Not in** your workspace's name is one your role there can't see, so you won't get it from that workspace.

- You're only told about what you can see: for example, payments need permission to read invoices.
- You're never told about something you did yourself.
- Notifications go to every phone you're signed in on. Signing out stops them on that phone.
- The lock screen shows the client's name, the document number and the amount. To hide notification text on the lock screen, change it in your phone's notification settings.

## Tracking time

The round **timer** button in the middle of the bar and the **Time** tab show when your plan includes [time tracking](/docs/work/time-tracking) and your role can track time.

### The timer

Tap the **timer** button to open the timer:

- **Start a timer**: type what you're working on, tap **No project** to pick a project (or a recent piece of work), switch **Billable** if you need to (it follows the project otherwise), then **Start**. While it runs you can change the description, the project or billable; changes save straight away.
- **Start a timer at once**: long-press the **timer** button. A blank timer starts; tap **Add details** on the message to fill it in.
- **Keep using the app**: swipe the timer down (iPhone), tap **⌄** at the top or go back. It shrinks to a bar above the tabs with what you're working on and the time so far, and keeps running while you use the rest of the app. Tap the bar (or swipe it up) to open the timer again. While a timer runs, the **timer** button shows the time too.
- **Stop**: tap **Stop** on the timer or the stop button on the bar. The time is logged for today. Stopped by mistake? Tap **Undo** on the message and the timer carries on from when it started.
- **Favourites**: listed on the timer (**Switch to** while one runs). Tap one to start it at once (it stops any running timer). Pin favourites on the web, from the timer bar's star.

### The Time tab

The **Time** tab shows your entries:

- **Pick a day** on the week strip at the top. Each day shows its total; **‹ ›** go to the week before or after. **Day** lists that day's entries; **Week** lists the whole week, day by day.
- Tap **▶** next to an entry to continue it as a new timer. The running one shows **Running**; tap it to open the timer.
- **Log time**: forgot the timer? Tap **Log time** (bottom right) for the day you picked. Enter a **Duration** (type `1:30`, `90m` or `1.5`, or tap **15m** to **8h**) or switch to **Start and end** and pick the times. Add what you worked on, the project and billable. Billable follows the project unless you change it.
- **Edit or delete**: tap an entry to change its description, project, billable, and its start and end (in your workspace's time zone; an end before the start means it ends the next day). Entries logged as a duration show the duration instead. **Delete** asks before it removes anything. Closing with unsaved changes asks first.

Invoiced entries, time before the workspace's [lock date](/docs/work/time-tracking#time-rules) and time on a submitted or approved timesheet open read-only, with the reason.

The timer runs on Billleaf's servers, not on your phone: close the app, lock your phone or switch to the web, and it keeps counting. You have one timer at a time, shared with the web.

### Forgotten timer reminder

When a timer has been running for **8 hours**, your phone shows a notification asking whether it's still running. To change when, or turn it off, pick **Off**, **4 h**, **6 h**, **8 h** or **10 h** under **Timer reminder** on the **Account** screen (the line at the bottom of the **Time** tab opens it too). The choice is kept on that phone, for every workspace. It works for a timer you started on the web too, as long as you've opened the app since. Tap the notification to open the timer, or long-press it (swipe down on Android) and tap **Stop timer** to stop it there and then. The entry ends when you tap it, so fix the end time if you stopped work earlier.

- The app asks to send notifications the first time it sees a running timer. If you said no, turn notifications on for Billleaf in your phone's Settings.
- **Android:** the app also asks once to set alarms. Without that, Android can show the reminder hours late. To turn it on later, go to **Settings → Apps → Billleaf → Alarms & reminders** and turn on **Allow setting alarms and reminders**.
- Reminders are for the workspace you have open in the app. Switching workspace or signing out removes the reminder.
- A timer that's already past your reminder time when you open the app (or when you shorten it) gets no reminder: you can see it on screen.

### Your week

If your workspace uses [timesheet approvals](/docs/work/timesheet-approvals), switch the **Time** tab to **Week** to see the week you picked: its total and where it stands.

- **Not submitted**: tap **Submit for approval** when the week is complete. Stop a running timer first.
- **Waiting for approval**: the week's time can't change. Tap **Withdraw** to take it back and make changes.
- **Sent back**: the approver's note says what to change. Fix the time, then tap **Submit again**.
- **Approved**: the week is locked. Ask an approver to reopen it if something needs to change.

To submit an earlier week, go back to it with **‹** on the week strip.

### Timesheet approvals

If your workspace uses [timesheet approvals](/docs/work/timesheet-approvals) and you can approve, the top of the **Time** tab shows **Timesheet approvals** with how many weeks are waiting for you (it's also under **More**). Tap it to see the weeks, filtered by **Waiting**, **Approved**, **Sent back** or **All**. Tap a week to see its time by project and by day, then:

- **Approve** it, with a note if you like.
- **Send back** the week with a note saying what to change. The member gets an email and can fix the week.
- **Reopen week** (an approved week), with a note, so the member can change it again.

For the calendar, the weekly timesheet, tags and other people's time, use [time tracking on the web](/docs/work/time-tracking).

## Invoices and payments

**Invoices** (in **More** on a phone, or its own tab without time tracking) shows who owes you what. It opens on **Unpaid**: overdue invoices first, then the rest by due date, with the total still due. Switch to **Overdue**, **Drafts**, **Paid** or **All** with the buttons at the top; pull down to refresh.

Tap an invoice to see its items, totals, credit notes and payments. From there:

- **Record payment**: money came in? Enter the amount (the balance is filled in; change it for a part payment), the date and how you were paid, with a reference if you like. Billleaf creates the [receipt](/docs/getting-paid/payments-and-receipts) as it does on the web, then offers to email it to the client.
- **Share PDF**: send the invoice from your phone with Mail, Messages or any other app, save it to Files or print it.
- **Email client**: emails the invoice with its PDF, as **Send** does on the web. **To** starts as the client's primary contact (you can change it), and you can add a message.
- **Send receipt**: under each payment, emails its receipt the same way. The payment shows when the receipt was last sent. Refunded and voided payments' receipts can't be sent.
- **Void**: under each payment, for one recorded by mistake. Give a reason; the amount goes back on the invoice and the receipt is marked void (see [voiding a payment](/docs/getting-paid/payments-and-receipts#voiding-a-payment-recorded-by-mistake)).

If the client has no email address, type one in **To**. Leave **Save as … email** on to keep it on the client for next time (your role needs to allow editing clients).

You only see the buttons your [role](/docs/getting-started/team) allows. Creating, editing and issuing invoices, and refunds, happen on the web.

### Quotes

Open **More → Quotes**, or switch to **Quotes** at the top of **Invoices**, for your [quotes](/docs/selling/quotes). **Waiting** shows sent quotes the client hasn't answered yet, with when each one expires (or that it has). **Drafts**, **Accepted**, **Declined** and **All** show the rest. The tab is called **Quotes** if your role can see quotes but not invoices.

Tap a quote to see its total, items, notes and terms, and the next step:

- **Mark as sent** (a draft): gives the quote its number and locks it, as on the web. The app asks first.
- **Share PDF** and **Email client**: as for invoices. The email has a link where the client can accept or decline.
- **Accepted** / **Declined** (a sent quote): record an answer the client gave you outside the client portal. Answers given in the portal show by themselves, with who answered.
- **Create invoice** (an accepted quote): a draft invoice with the quote's items, opened right away. Review and issue it on the web. The quote then links to it.
- **Start project** (an accepted quote): a fixed-price project budgeted at the quote total.

A client's screen lists their latest quotes too. Writing and editing quotes, contracts from quotes and duplicating happen on the web.

### Contracts

Open **More → Contracts**, or switch to **Contracts** at the top of **Invoices**, for your [contracts](/docs/selling/contracts). **Waiting** shows contracts sent and not signed yet; **Drafts**, **Signed** and **All** show the rest. The tab is called **Contracts** if your role can see contracts but not invoices or quotes.

Tap a contract to read it, laid out as on the PDF, with who it's with, its dates and where it stands. Then:

- **Mark as sent** (a draft): gives the contract its number and freezes its text, as on the web. If a merge field has no value yet (it's highlighted in the text), fill it in on the web first.
- **Share PDF** and **Email client**: as for invoices. The email has a link where the client reads and signs the contract in the client portal.
- **Record signature** (a sent contract): for a signature you got on paper or by email. Enter who signed and when. Signatures given in the portal show by themselves.
- **Client declined** (a sent contract): records that the client won't sign.
- **Void contract** (a sent or signed contract): it stops being in force but stays on record. You can add a reason.

The contract links to its client, and to the quote and project it belongs to. Writing and editing contracts and templates happen on the web.

## Clients and projects

**Clients** (in **More** on a phone) lists your active [clients](/docs/selling/clients) from A to Z. Search by name or email; pull down to refresh. Switch to **Projects** at the top for your [projects](/docs/work/projects). The tab shows when your [role](/docs/getting-started/team) can see clients or projects (it's called **Projects** if you can only see projects).

Tap a client to see:

- **Call** and **Email**: open your phone or mail app with the client's number or address.
- **Owes you**: what's still due on the client's issued invoices, per currency, and how many are overdue. **Unpaid** lists those invoices, overdue first; **Recent invoices** shows the latest of the rest, drafts included. Tap one to open it (your role needs to see invoices).
- **Contacts**: each contact's role, email and phone. Tap an email or number to write or call.
- **Details**: email, phone, website, address (tap it to open Maps), tax ID, and the currency and payment terms new invoices use.
- **Quotes** and **Contracts**: the client's latest, with where each stands.
- **Projects** for the client, with the time tracked on each.
- **Notes** you've kept on the client.

On an invoice, tap the client's name to open the client.

### Projects

**Projects** lists **Active**, **On hold** or **Completed** projects from A to Z, with the client, the due date and the time tracked so far. Search by name. Tap a project to see:

- **Start timer**: starts the timer on this project and opens the **Time** tab. If a timer is already running on something else, the app asks first, then stops it. Shown when you can track time.
- **Tasks**: in progress first, then to do, each with its assignee and due date (red when overdue). Tick a task to mark it done; **Show done** lists finished ones, where you can untick them. Tap a task to **Start timer** on it, or to move it between **To do** and **In progress**. **Add a task** at the bottom adds one to To do. Changing and adding tasks needs a role that can edit projects; assigning, due dates, notes and reordering happen on the web.
- **Time**: tracked, billable, and not invoiced yet (with its value, for hourly projects).
- **Invoice unbilled time**: on an hourly project with a client and time not invoiced yet, creates a draft invoice for that time, as on the web, and opens it. Review and issue it on the web. Your role needs to create invoices and see time.
- **Budget**: time and money used against the project's estimates. The percentage turns red over 100%.
- **Details**: billing, dates, and the quote the project came from. Then the description.

Tap the client's name to open the client. Adding and editing clients, contacts and projects happen on the web.

## Cashbook

The **Money** tab holds your [cashbooks](/docs/books/cashbook) and your expenses (switch between them at the top). If you can only see one of them, the tab is named after it.

- **Your books** lists each book with its balance, when it last changed and who else is in it, most recently used first. **Show archived** lists archived books. Tap **Add book** (bottom right) and give it a name and a currency.
- **Open a book** to see the **net balance**, **total in** and **total out**, and the entries grouped by day: the remark with its payment mode and category, who added it and when, the amount (**+** in green, **−** in red) and the balance after it. Pick **Today**, **This month** and so on, search a remark or amount, or open **Filters** for a custom range, cash in or out, a category, payment mode or who added it. **Clear filters** shows everything again.
- **Cash in** and **Cash out** at the bottom open the entry form with the amount ready to type. Switch between in and out at the top; pick the date and time with your phone's own pickers, add a remark, contact, category or payment mode and photos if you like. **Save & add new** keeps the form open for the next one. The category and mode you used last are picked for you. Closing the form with unsaved changes asks first.
- **Tap an entry** for its details and history; **Edit** or **Delete** it if your role allows. **Long-press** entries to select several, then **Delete** them or **Move or copy** them to another book in the same currency.
- **Reports** (the chart icon, or **View report**) shows a period's opening and closing balance, totals, and the entries by day, category or payment mode. **Share PDF** sends the statement; **CSV** gives a file for Excel.
- **Settings** (the gear): rename the book, turn entry fields on or off, add, rename, hide or delete categories and payment modes (the **⋯** next to each), manage members and their roles, archive or delete the book, or leave it.
- On a wide tablet, your books and the open book sit side by side.

## Expenses and receipts

The **Expenses** pane of the **Money** tab lists your latest [expenses](/docs/books/expenses) by month, newest first, with each month's total. If you use several categories, tap one at the top to see only those. Expenses without a receipt say **No receipt**. Tap an expense to see its details and open its receipt; to change or delete it, tap **Edit on the web**.

To record one, tap **Add expense** (bottom right):

1. **Take photo** (or **Gallery**) to attach the receipt first. The app asks for camera or photo access the first time. Retake or remove it from the photo.
2. Enter the **amount**, tax included, in your workspace's default currency. A comma or a point works as the decimal separator.
3. Check the **date** (today unless you change it), and add who you paid, a category, how you paid and a note if you like.
4. Tap **Save expense and receipt**. Closing with something entered asks first.

Photos are saved as JPEGs and shrunk to a readable size before they upload, so they stay under the 5 MB limit. If the receipt can't be uploaded, the expense is still saved and you can attach the receipt on the web.

Other currencies, tax amounts, clients and projects, billable expenses and editing all happen on the web.
