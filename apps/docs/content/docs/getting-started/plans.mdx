## Paying for a plan

Billleaf plans are paid in advance, by bank transfer or mobile wallet:

<Steps>
<Step>
Go to **Settings → Billing** and choose a plan and period (monthly or yearly).
</Step>
<Step>
Pay the amount shown to one of Billleaf's accounts (bank, wallet or QR code).
</Step>
<Step>
Upload a screenshot of the payment and its transaction ID.
</Step>
<Step>
We check it and switch your plan on. You get an email when it's approved — or, if we can't match the payment, an email saying why so you can send a new screenshot.
</Step>
</Steps>

Paying again before your period ends adds the new period to the end of the current one.

## When a plan ends

Plans don't renew automatically. We email the workspace owners 7 days and 1 day before the end. If a paid period ends, the workspace keeps working for 7 more days, then moves to the Free plan's limits. **Nothing is deleted** — you just can't, for example, add a sixth client until you renew.
