## Forgot your password?

On the sign-in page, choose **Forgot password?**, enter your email, and open the link we send you. It works once and for an hour. Choose a new password (at least 10 characters) and sign in with it. You're signed out on your other devices.

For privacy, the page says "Check your email" whether or not that address has an account. If nothing arrives, check spam, or ask for a new link.

## Two-factor authentication

Two-factor authentication (2FA) asks for a 6-digit code from an authenticator app (such as Google Authenticator, 1Password or Authy) each time you sign in on a new device.

<Steps>
<Step>
Open your account menu (bottom of the sidebar) → **Account** → **Two-factor authentication**.
</Step>
<Step>
Enter your password, then scan the QR code with your authenticator app.
</Step>
<Step>
Type the code the app shows to confirm, and save your **backup codes** somewhere safe. Each backup code works once if you lose your phone.
</Step>
</Steps>

Keep your backup codes: without your phone or a backup code, you can't sign in.
