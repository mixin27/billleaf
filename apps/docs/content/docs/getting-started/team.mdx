title: Team and roles
description: Invite people and decide what each of them can do.
---

Go to **Settings → Members** to invite people by email. They get a link to join; if they're new to Billleaf they create an account first. The number of members depends on your [plan](/docs/getting-started/plans).

## Built-in roles

| Role | Can do |
| --- | --- |
| **Owner** | Everything, including billing, exporting data and deleting the workspace. A workspace always has at least one owner. |
| **Admin** | Everything except billing, data export and deleting the workspace. |
| **Member** | Works on clients, quotes, contracts, projects, time and expenses. Can't create or send invoices, record payments, manage billing or see cost rates. |
| **Accountant** | Read access to everything, plus invoices, payments, refunds and expenses. |

## Custom roles (Team)

On the Team plan, **Settings → Roles & permissions** lets you create your own roles by ticking exactly which actions they allow — for example "can track time and view projects, nothing else".

## Removing someone

Removing a member signs them out of the workspace. Their tracked time stays, under their name, and anything they created stays too. API keys they created stop working.
