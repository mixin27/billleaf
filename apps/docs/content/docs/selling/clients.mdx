---
title: Clients
description: Keep each client's details, contacts and history in one place.
---

Go to **Clients → New client**. A client can be a company or a person.

- **Billing details** — legal name, email, address and tax ID appear on documents you send them.
- **Currency and payment terms** — leave empty to use your workspace defaults, or set them per client (for example a client who pays in EUR on 30-day terms).
- **Contacts** — add the people you deal with. The **primary contact** receives documents by default.

## The client page

Everything for one client lives on their page: invoices, quotes, projects, expenses to bill, and contacts. Start a new quote, invoice or project from there and the client is filled in for you.

## Archiving

Archive clients you no longer work with. They disappear from pickers and lists but keep their history. Archived clients don't count towards the Free plan's 5 active clients. A client with documents can't be deleted, only archived.
