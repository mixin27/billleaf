## Templates

**Contracts → Templates** holds reusable agreement text. A standard services agreement is included to start from. Use **merge fields** in double braces — they're filled in when you draft a contract:

| Field | Becomes |
| --- | --- |
| `{{client.name}}`, `{{client.legal_name}}`, `{{client.address}}`, `{{client.tax_id}}` | The client's details |
| `{{business.name}}`, `{{business.legal_name}}`, `{{business.address}}` | Your details |
| `{{quote.number}}`, `{{quote.title}}`, `{{quote.total}}` | The linked quote |
| `{{contract.effective_date}}`, `{{contract.end_date}}`, `{{contract.number}}` | The contract's dates and number |
| `{{project.name}}`, `{{today}}` | The linked project, the date it's sent |

Simple formatting works: `**bold**`, `## headings` and lists.

## Sending and signing

<Steps>
<Step>
Draft a contract from a template (or from an accepted quote) and review the text. Fields that couldn't be filled are highlighted — you can't send until they're resolved.
</Step>
<Step>
**Mark as sent**. The contract gets its number (CON-0001, …) and its text is frozen. Then **Email** it: your client gets the PDF and a link to read and sign it in the client portal.
</Step>
<Step>
They sign by typing their name. Billleaf records the name, time, email, IP address and browser as evidence, and adds a signature certificate page to the PDF.
</Step>
</Steps>

If a client signs on paper, use **Record signature** instead. Signed contracts can't be changed; void one and draft a new version if needed.

You can do all of this from the [mobile app](/docs/getting-started/mobile-app#contracts) too, except writing and editing.
