## Create and send

<Steps>
<Step>
**Quotes → New quote**: pick the client, add lines (description, quantity, price, tax rate) and an expiry date. Notes and terms are optional.
</Step>
<Step>
Click **Send**. The quote gets its number (for example `QUO-0007`) and is emailed with a PDF and a link to the [client portal](/docs/client-portal).
</Step>
</Steps>

Numbers are given in order with no gaps when a quote is sent, not when it's drafted.

## Accepted or declined

Your client can **accept** (typing their name) or **decline** (with an optional reason) from the portal. You're emailed either way. If they reply by email instead, record it yourself from the quote's **More** menu.

## After acceptance

From an accepted quote you can:

- **Create invoice** — copies the lines into a new draft invoice (once per quote).
- **Start project** — creates a fixed-price project budgeted at the quote total.
- **Draft contract** — fills a contract template with the quote's details.

Sent quotes can't be edited. To change one, duplicate it and send the new version.

## On your phone

In the [mobile app](/docs/getting-started/mobile-app#quotes), quotes are in the **Invoices** tab. You can mark a draft as sent, email or share the PDF, record the client's answer, and turn an accepted quote into an invoice or a project.
