title: Rates and budgets
description: What your time is worth, what it costs, and how much is left.

## Billable rates

Time on an hourly project is priced with the **most specific** rate that's set:

1. the **task**'s rate (from the task's menu on the project board),
2. the **project**'s rate,
3. the **member**'s rate (**Settings → Rates**),
4. the workspace **default rate** (**Settings → Rates**).

Member and default rates are in your workspace's default currency and only apply to projects in that currency — nothing is converted. When time is invoiced, each entry keeps the rate it was invoiced at, so changing a rate later doesn't change past invoices.

## Cost and profit

Give members a **cost rate** — what an hour of their time costs you. Projects and time reports then show cost and profit alongside billable amounts. Only people with permission to see rates (owners, admins and accountants by default) see costs.

## Estimates and alerts

On a project, set a **time estimate** (hours) and, for hourly projects, a **budget** (money). The project page shows progress bars that turn amber at 80% and red at 100%, and **Home** lists the projects closest to their estimates. Workspace owners are emailed once when a project reaches 80% and again at 100%.
