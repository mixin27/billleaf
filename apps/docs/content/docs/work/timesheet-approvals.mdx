title: Timesheet approvals
description: Members submit each week of time; an approver signs it off before it can be invoiced.
---

On the **Team** plan, you can have someone check each person's week of time before it's billed. Turn it on in **Settings → General → Timesheet approvals**.

## How it works

1. A member logs their week as usual: timer, calendar or timesheet.
2. On **Time → Timesheet**, they press **Submit for approval**. Approvers get an email.
3. An approver opens **Time → Approvals**, reviews the week, and either:
   - **Approves** it, optionally with a note. The member gets an email.
   - **Sends it back** with a note saying what to change. The member gets an email, fixes the week and submits it again.

While a week is **waiting** or **approved**, its time is locked: nobody can add, change or delete entries in it, from the timer, calendar, timesheet or an import. A member can **withdraw** a week that's still waiting. To change an approved week, an approver **reopens** it (with a note), which sends it back to the member.

You can't submit a week that hasn't started yet, a week with no time, or a week with a timer still running.

Approvers can also review weeks in the [mobile app](/docs/getting-started/mobile-app#timesheet-approvals): the **Time** tab shows how many are waiting, and a **Timesheet submitted** notification opens the week.

## Invoicing

With approvals on, **Invoice unbilled time** on a project only takes time from **approved** weeks. The project shows how much unbilled time is still waiting for approval. Time from people who have left the workspace counts as approved.

If approvals are turned off, or the plan no longer includes them, weeks aren't locked and all time can be invoiced again. Past approvals are kept.

## Who can approve

**Owner** and **Admin** can approve by default. To let someone else approve, give their role the permission **Approve or reject submitted timesheets** in **Settings → Roles & permissions**. Approvers can approve their own week, so a solo owner is never stuck.

## For developers

Webhooks send `timesheet.submitted`, `timesheet.approved` and `timesheet.rejected` (sent back or reopened). See [Webhooks](/docs/developers/webhooks).
