# Product Vision

> Billleaf — product vision

## One sentence

Billleaf is an all-in-one workspace for freelancers to manage clients, agreements, projects, time, and finances — from the first quotation to the final receipt.

**Tagline:** _Your work. Your business. One place._

## Problem

Freelancers stitch together a spreadsheet for clients, a doc template for quotes, a PDF tool for contracts, a timer app, an invoicing app and a bank statement. Data is re-typed at every step, numbering drifts, and nobody knows what is still unpaid.

## Promise

Spend less time on business administration and more time on client work. Information entered once flows forward:

```
Client → Quote → Contract → Project → Time → Invoice → Payment → Receipt
```

## Who it is for

| Stage | Audience                                                               | Needs                                                               |
| ----- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Now   | Solo service freelancers (developers, designers, writers, consultants) | Fast documents, correct numbering, multi-currency, "what's unpaid?" |
| Later | Small studios and agencies                                             | Shared workspace, roles & permissions, approvals, client portal     |
| Later | Mobile-first freelancers                                               | Track time and send invoices from a phone (Expo app)                |

## Product principles

1. **Connected, not bundled.** Every module links to the previous stage of the journey; no duplicate entry.
2. **Correct by default.** Money in minor units, immutable issued documents, gap-free per-workspace numbering, audit trail.
3. **Calm UI.** Minimal, professional, keyboard-friendly; generous whitespace.
4. **Workspace-first.** A solo freelancer is simply a workspace of one — the same model scales to agencies without migration.
5. **Own your data.** Exports (CSV/PDF) for everything; no lock-in.

## Non-goals (for now)

- Full double-entry accounting or tax filing (integrate instead).
- Payroll, HR, inventory.
- A generic project-management tool competing with Jira/Linear.

## Success signals

- Time from sign-up to first sent invoice < 10 minutes.
- % of invoices created from a quote/project/time entries (connectedness).
- Free → Pro conversion and 3-month retention.
